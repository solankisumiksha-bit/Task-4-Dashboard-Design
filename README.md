# 📊 Sales & Financial Performance Dashboard

## Task 4 – Dashboard Design

An interactive **Sales & Financial Performance Dashboard** created using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales, profit, orders, products, categories, regions and order status.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw sales and financial data into an interactive dashboard that provides useful business insights and supports data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

* Microsoft Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX
* CSV Dataset
* Microsoft PowerPoint

---

## 📂 Dataset

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

1. Total sales reached ₹43.54 lakh with ₹11.92 lakh profit.
2. Overall profit margin was 27.37%.
3. West was the highest-sales region with ₹16.08 lakh.
4. Electronics was the largest category with ₹30.49 lakh sales.
5. Laptop was the top-selling product with ₹15.78 lakh sales.
6. November recorded the highest monthly sales at ₹7.68 lakh.
7. July recorded the lowest monthly sales at ₹54,678.
8. Accessories had the highest category profit margin at approximately 38.48%.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Focus inventory planning on high-performing Electronics products.
* Maintain sufficient stock for Laptop and Smartphone products.
* Investigate the sales decline observed in July.
* Replicate successful strategies from the West region.
* Increase focus on high-margin categories such as Accessories.
* Monitor processing and cancelled orders to improve operational efficiency.
